# Brand and content system

## Position

McCanney Capital is the answer-first retirement and risk-strategy practice. Its job is to make the decision legible before recommending a product or broader engagement.

Core formulation:

> Numbers first. Pressure never.

Primary homepage promise:

> You don't need another pitch. You need an answer.

## Voice

Mark sounds like an experienced practitioner at a table with a client:

- Lead with the short answer or the first useful calculation.
- Use plain English and explain the contract in the client's terms.
- Name assumptions and what remains unknown.
- Put costs, constraints, alternatives, and failure cases in equal type.
- Treat “keep it,” “wait,” and “do nothing” as valid recommendations.
- Prefer firm, dry clarity to corporate warmth or combative outrage.

Avoid “financial journey,” “pursue your dreams,” “holistic solutions,” “peace of mind,” and generic lifestyle language unless a sentence supplies concrete meaning.

## Practice-versus-publication tone

- The main practice site should feel calm, credible, and operational.
- Media can be sharper, more opinionated, and more openly critical of bad industry behavior.
- Both share the same values, palette, typographic structure, and respect for the reader.
- Do not duplicate the whole article library into this site. Link to the publication.

## Interaction pattern

- Ask only for inputs materially required by the question.
- Show the result immediately and identify its limits.
- Keep calculators educational and avoid false precision.
- Do not collect or transmit personal information silently.
- Any future form must state what happens after submission and how quickly a human will respond.

## Visual system

- Warm paper: `#f3f0e7`
- Near-black ink: `#151612`
- Signal red: `#f14c2e`
- Lime: `#d8f44f`
- Powder blue: `#9ccbd8`
- Lavender: `#cbbcf1`
- Yellow: `#f4c957`

Use editorial hierarchy, serif/sans contrast, strong rules, asymmetric grids, and restrained primary-color accents. The portrait should feel documentary, not aspirational. Avoid glossy gradients, finance icons, coins, handshakes, sailboats, beaches, cardigans, and posed retirees.

## Accuracy and compliance

- Verify all licenses, registrations, memberships, service states, years, counts, addresses, phone numbers, compensation statements, and testimonials before the custom-domain launch.
- Do not quote current rates, tax limits, laws, ratings, or product features from memory.
- Keep insurance guarantees distinct from nonguaranteed illustrations and advisory projections.
- Label tools as educational estimates and explain material omissions.
- Do not publish private client facts or testimonials without permission and required disclosures.
